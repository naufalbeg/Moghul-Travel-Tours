# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Malaysian Muslims, mostly in their 40s–60s: families, retired couples, first-time
and returning Umrah pilgrims, and small groups (family, office, surau). They read
in Malay, browse mostly on phones (often from a link someone shared on WhatsApp),
and decide after talking to a person. Many are not confident with web forms.

## Product Purpose

The public website of Moghul Travel & Tours Sdn Bhd, plus the admin portal its
staff use to keep it current. A visitor should be able to find a trip, see what it
includes and what it starts from, and reach the agency about it. Success is a
visitor contacting the agency (WhatsApp, phone, inquiry or booking request) about
a specific package or trip. Payment and confirmation happen offline.

## Positioning

Muslim-friendly travel ("perjalanan mesra Muslim") from one small, licensed,
owner-run agency in Shah Alam: Umrah & Ziarah and halal-friendly group tours
abroad and at home, handled personally by the same team for about a decade.
Pilgrimage and holidays sit under one umbrella; neither is a side business.

## Operating Context

- WhatsApp first (012-588 5590), then phone; office at Seksyen 8, Shah Alam,
  Monday–Friday office hours.
- Packages change often and are priced "Bermula dari RM X seorang" because
  airfares move; each package has a per-type price list and departure dates.
- Staff edit packages, banner photos, gallery, testimonials, announcements and
  site text themselves; the design must survive whatever they type and upload.
- Moghul is also a certified Muslim Travel Bug agent; more packages live on that
  portal ("Pakej Lain").

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Malay-first with a BM | EN switch on the same URLs; admin portal is English.
- Categories: Umrah & Ziarah, Luar Negara, Dalam Negara, Cruise.
- Package pages carry an inquiry form and, when dates and prices allow, a
  booking request form ("Borang Tempahan"). No payment gateway.
- Built in Next.js 16 + Tailwind v4; content comes from the database per request.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Moghul Travel & Tours (legal: Moghul Travel & Tours Sdn Bhd).
- Logo: globe, orange swoosh and plane. Its blue (#1B5FA8 family) and orange
  (#F07C1E family) stay the brand colours.
- Everyday Malaysian Malay; common English terms kept (Cruise, Admin login).
- "About a decade" of operation; never larger claims.
- Footer credit "Developed by MNB".

## Evidence on Hand

- About 49 real customer trip photos in the gallery: Mecca, Hunza (Pakistan),
  Petra (Jordan), Lombok, Da Nang, Vietnam, Cambodia, Switzerland.
- Licences and memberships: MOTAC KPK/LN 9109, Co. Reg. 1273862-K,
  MATTA MA 5895, MITA A924, PAPUH PP 276; certified Muslim Travel Bug agent.
- About a dozen published packages with real prices, durations and highlights
  (currently all Luar Negara: Türkiye, Phu Quoc, Bangkok, Hainan, Jakarta +
  Bandung, Da Nang, Surabaya + Bromo, Taipei).
- Owner, team and office photos exist but are not on the site yet; mockups mark
  where they go.
- Real customer reviews exist (customers, Google, Facebook) but none are on the
  site yet. Never invent one; mockups use clearly labelled placeholders.
- No Umrah package is published at the moment.

## Product Principles

1. A real person is always one tap away: WhatsApp and phone stay visible.
2. Prove with Moghul's own trips, photos and licences, not generic travel claims.
3. Legible and calm for a 60-year-old on a phone.
4. Honest prices ("Bermula dari"), no fake urgency or invented numbers.
5. Muslim-friendly is the umbrella; pilgrimage and holidays share it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Older audience: body text 17px or larger, touch targets 48px or larger, strong
contrast, nothing that moves on its own except the pausable banner fade, reduced
motion respected, Malay `lang` with English sections marked.
