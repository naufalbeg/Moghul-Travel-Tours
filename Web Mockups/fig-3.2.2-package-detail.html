Umrah Sakinah — 10 days

10 days, 9 nights Twin / triple sharing MOTAC licensed package

About this package

A gentle, well-paced Umrah journey through Makkah and Madinah, designed with families and older travellers in mind. We keep hotels close to the Haramain to minimise walking, and our group leader is with you throughout — from the airport to your final ziarah stop.

What's included

Return flights from Kuala Lumpur
4-star hotels near the Haramain
Daily halal breakfast & dinner
Visa & Umrah permit processing
Experienced group leader & ustaz
Airport & inter-city transfers

Itinerary

Day 1

Depart Kuala Lumpur → Madinah

Group check-in and departure. Arrival in Madinah, transfer to hotel and rest.

Day 2–4

Madinah — Masjid Nabawi

Daily prayers at Masjid Nabawi, guided ziarah to key historical sites around Madinah.

Day 5

Madinah → Makkah

Depart for Makkah by coach, Umrah performed together as a group upon arrival.

Day 6–8

Makkah — Masjid al-Haram

Daily prayers at Masjid al-Haram, guided ziarah around Makkah, free time for personal worship.

Day 9

Free day & farewell Tawaf

Rest, last-minute shopping in Makkah, and a farewell Tawaf before departure preparations.

Day 10

Makkah → Kuala Lumpur

Transfer to Jeddah airport for the return flight home.
